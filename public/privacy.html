Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deadclock ("Deadclock," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Deadclock desktop app, the Deadclock iPhone app, and deadclock.gg (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information you give us

  • Support requests. When you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from inside an app, we receive the text you submit and when you sent it.

You don't need an account to use Deadclock.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics. The apps, and the platforms they run on, may collect information such as app opens, screens viewed, features used, crashes, and performance data. On iPhone this is collected through Google Firebase Analytics. On Windows, the Overwolf platform collects analytics as part of running the app.
  • Device and technical data. Such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate region, and IP address.
  • Advertising data. Where ads are shown, our advertising partners may collect device or advertising identifiers, as described in Section 4.
  • Website. deadclock.gg is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. The website itself does not set cookies or use analytics.

3. Game data

The desktop app uses game event data made available through Overwolf, such as match time and match state, to run your timers. This data is used on your device to provide the app's features. We do not use it to identify you.

4. Ads

Free versions of Deadclock may show ads.

  • iPhone. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, you'll be asked for consent before ads load.
  • Windows. Ads are served through Overwolf's advertising platform and its partners, which may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies, subject to your consent choices where required.

Advertising partners process information under their own privacy policies, linked in Section 9.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Overwolf. We never receive or store your payment card details. We may receive limited information needed to confirm a purchase, such as whether it is active.

6. Information stored on your device

Your settings, preferences, and app logs are stored on your device. Local logs stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us.

7. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Services
  • Keep timer data up to date
  • Respond to support requests and feedback
  • Show ads and process purchases
  • Understand usage, fix bugs, and monitor performance
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

8. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us run the Services, such as Google, Apple, Overwolf, and Cloudflare
  • With advertising partners, as described in Section 4
  • When required by law, or when we believe it's necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Deadclock, our users, or others
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information
  • With your consent

9. Third-party services

These providers process information under their own policies:

  • Apple: Privacy Policy
  • Google (Firebase and AdMob): Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy, How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Overwolf: Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

10. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law. Support emails and feedback are kept as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record keeping. Information held by third parties is kept under their policies.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Ads and consent. Use the consent options shown in the apps and your device's privacy settings to manage how ads use your information.
  • Stop collection. You can stop all collection by the apps by uninstalling them.
  • Privacy rights. Depending on where you live, including the EEA, UK, and certain US states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email support@deadclock.gg. We may need to verify your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Complaints. If you're in the EEA or UK, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Where EEA or UK law applies, we process information based on your consent (such as for ads where required), our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Services, the performance of a contract with you (such as purchases), and compliance with legal obligations.

13. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. International users

Deadclock is operated from the United States. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here and change the date at the top. If you keep using the Services after an update, you accept the updated policy.
