Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deadclock ("Deadclock," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Deadclock desktop app, the Deadclock iPhone app, and deadclock.gg (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information you give us

  • Support requests. When you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from inside an app, we receive the text you submit and when you sent it.

You don't need an account to use Deadclock.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics. The apps, and the platforms they run on, may collect information such as app opens, screens viewed, features used, crashes, and performance data. On iPhone this is collected through Google Firebase Analytics. On Windows, the Overwolf platform collects analytics as part of running the app. The Windows app keeps this to the minimum Overwolf requires, but it can't be switched off. The Windows app does not use Firebase Analytics.
  • Device and technical data. Such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate region, and IP address.
  • Advertising data. Where ads are shown, our advertising partners may collect device or advertising identifiers, as described in Section 4.
  • Website. deadclock.gg is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. The website itself does not set cookies or use analytics.

3. Game data and the Windows app's network requests

The Windows app is the Deadclock desktop app that runs on Overwolf. It uses game data made available through Overwolf. Match time and match state, which run your timers, are used on your device. Player identifiers are handled differently, as described next.

Lobby scouting

When a Deadlock match starts, the Windows app reads the Steam account ID of every player in the match, yours included (up to 12 players). It sends those IDs to the Deadlock API (api.deadlock-api.com) to look up each player's rank and hero statistics from the last 30 days, and shows the results in the app window. This feature is always on. The app has no setting to turn it off. Uninstalling the app stops it.

  • Steam account IDs are public identifiers. Each one is tied to a Steam profile. They belong to the other players in your match as well as to you.
  • The Deadlock API is a third party. It is an independent community project run by volunteers, and it is not operated by Valve, Overwolf, or Deadclock. Its server receives the account IDs and your IP address. See deadlock-api.com.
  • Deadclock doesn't receive this data. We don't operate a server that receives the IDs or the results. The app keeps results in memory for about 15 minutes. It doesn't save them to disk or send them anywhere else.

Other requests the Windows app makes

These requests don't carry player or account data. The servers that answer them can see your IP address.

  • Hero images. The app window loads the list of heroes from the Deadlock API (api.deadlock-api.com/v1/assets/heroes) and downloads the hero images from its asset host.
  • Remote configuration. When the app starts, it reads timer definitions and the hero list from Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase). This is an anonymous read of project data, and no user data is sent with it. The Windows app does not use Firebase Analytics.
  • Updates. The app checks Overwolf's update servers (electron-updates.overwolf.com) for new versions. Like any download, this reveals your IP address and the app version.

  • iPhone. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, you'll be asked for consent before ads load.

Ads are served through Overwolf's advertising platform and its partners, which may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies, subject to your consent choices where required. The Windows app shows Overwolf's consent screen. Where required, you can manage your ad privacy choices later in the app's Settings, through Overwolf's consent management.

Advertising partners process information under their own privacy policies, linked in Section 9.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple on iPhone and by Overwolf on Windows.

On Windows, a Premium plan may be offered in the future. If it is, it will be processed through Overwolf's subscriptions service, with Tebex as the merchant of record. At checkout, an identifier the app generates for your PC is sent to Overwolf and Tebex to link the purchase.

We never receive or store your payment card details. We may receive limited information needed to confirm a purchase, such as whether it is active.

6. Information stored on your device

Your settings, preferences, hotkeys, app logs, and the Windows app's crash log are stored on your device. They are not uploaded. Local logs stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us.
