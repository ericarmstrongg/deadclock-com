FAQ

Is Deadclock free?

Yes. Both apps are free to download. Optional upgrades, like removing ads, may be offered.

How do I install it on Windows?

Click Download for Windows, run the installer, and follow the steps.

Is there a Mac or Android version?

No. Deadclock is on Windows and iPhone.

The overlay isn't showing up.

Make sure the overlay is turned on in Deadclock's settings, then restart Deadlock. Still stuck? Email us.

A timer looks wrong.

Timers update automatically after game patches. If one still looks off, email us the timer name and what you saw.

How do I remove ads on iPhone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Remove Ads. On a new device, tap Restore Purchase.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Overwolf. Manage subscriptions and request refunds through them.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Email support@deadclock.gg and we'll take care of it.

Is Deadclock made by Valve?

No. Deadclock is an independent app and is not affiliated with Valve.
